Overlay+ — Welcome

Overlay+ is a compact, real-time social overlay for profiles, friends, direct messages, and group chats.

This page outlines all the powerful features built into the extension.

✨ Core Features

👤 Profile Management

  • Customization: Set your display name, avatar URL, status (Online, Idle, DND), and a personal description.
  • Live Preview: See exactly how your profile card looks to other players in real-time.
  • Sharing: Generate a unique link to share your profile with others, or save a PNG screenshot of your profile card directly to your device.

👥 Friends System

  • Add Friends: Send friend requests using a user's unique 16-character User ID.
  • Request Management: Easily accept or decline incoming friend requests.
  • Blocking: Block unwanted users to prevent them from interacting with you or sending requests.
  • Mutual Friends: See mutual connections when viewing another user's profile.

💬 Direct Messages (DMs)

  • Real-time Chat: Instant messaging with your friends, complete with live typing indicators and read receipts.
  • Message Search: Quickly find old messages within any conversation.
  • Pinning: Pin important conversations to the top of your sidebar.
  • Self-Chat & Bot: Use the "You" chat as a personal notepad, or view automated messages from the Mio System Bot.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Group Chats

  • Create Groups: Start a group chat with multiple friends at once.
  • @Mentions: Type @ to mention specific members, featuring autocomplete and visual highlighting.
  • Read Receipts: See exactly who has read your group messages ("Read by...").
  • Management: Group owners can rename the group, add members, or remove them.

⚙️ Settings & Banner

  • Banner Upload: Upload a custom banner image for your profile.
  • Built-in Cropper: Zoom, rotate, and flip your image to get the perfect 4:1 crop.
  • Positioning: Fine-tune the banner's X/Y position and scaling (Cover, Contain, Fill).

🛡️ Admin Panel (Role-based)

  • Access: Restricted to users with Administrator or Developer roles.
  • User Management: Search users by name or ID, and manage their roles.
  • Moderation: Ban or unban users with a required reason. Includes safeguards against self-banning and unauthorized role changes.
  • Slash Commands: Use /ban <userid> <reason>, /unban <userid>, and /role <userid> <role> directly in the panel.

🚀 How to Get Started

  1. Open the Overlay: Click the new profile label in the game modes selection. When it is active, press the green "Play" button to open the main panel.
  2. Set Up Your Profile: Go to the Profile tab, enter your name, avatar URL, description, and status, then click Save.
  3. Customize Your Banner: Head to the Settings tab to upload and crop a custom banner, then check the Preview tab to see the result.
  4. Connect with Others: Use the Friends tab to search for User IDs, send requests, and start chatting in the DMs section.

💡 Helpful Tips

  • Valid Image URLs: Ensure your avatar and banner URLs are direct links to images (e.g., ending in .png or .jpg) for them to display correctly.
  • Keep Names Clear: Short, readable names make it easier for friends to find and recognize you.
  • Real-time Sync: Changes to profiles, friend requests, and messages sync instantly via WebSocket. If something seems stuck, a quick page refresh usually resolves it.
  • Toast Notifications: Keep an eye on the top-right corner of your screen for pop-up notifications when you receive new messages or friend requests while the panel is closed.
